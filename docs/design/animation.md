# Seated posture and gestures

Issue #9. The four investor gestures, when each plays, and how reduced motion works. Clips are generated by `scripts/create-studio-avatar.mjs` under the rig contract (rotation-only, `RIG_CLIPS`). Poses come from `sceneCue` in `src/features/studio/model.ts` and are played by `Avatar` in `scene.tsx`. Enforced by `tests/studio-animation.test.ts`, `tests/studio-room-fit.test.ts` and `tests/studio.test.ts`.

## Gestures

| Clip | Length | When | Body | Face |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Listening | 2.8 s | Default; investors not asking | Small breath and attentive head tilt | Brows relax |
| Explaining (new) | 2.4 s | The investor whose question is open, during the question round | Open right-hand gesture, curious head tilt | Brows rise; jaw moves as if speaking |
| Offering | 1.5 s | After an opening offer or a counteroffer from that investor | Reaches across the desk, confident nod | Brows lift |
| Withdrawal | 1.7 s | After that investor passes or withdraws | Leans back, slow head shake | Frown: brows lower, inner ends down, and it stays |

All four clips only rotate bones, and the facial clips use the Issue #5 facial bones. The fit test samples nine frames of every clip at every seat and checks that no body vertex enters the chairs or the desk.

**Contract change.** `Explaining` was added after the three original clips. The existing test that lists the clips now requires all four, with the original three unchanged in name and order. This is stricter, not weaker.

## Where poses come from

`sceneCue` reads only the committed session:

- **Question round:** the investor whose question is open (`INVESTOR_ORDER[answers.length]`) is Explaining; the others are Listening. Each answered question changes the cue key, so the next investor's gesture plays.
- **After the questions:** each investor's last opening or counter decision picks Offering or Withdrawal, as before.

Drafts, camera choices, selection and animation completion never change the session or dispatch an action. A test checks that deriving poses leaves the session unchanged.

## No continuous idle loop

**Decision.** The studio renders on demand: frames are drawn only while something changes, which saves battery and CPU. A perpetual breathing loop would force continuous rendering on every device, and on the CI software renderer it would compete with the camera tests that had to be stabilised in Issue #11. Each gesture plays once when its cue changes and then holds its final pose.

## Reduced motion

With reduced motion, a gesture does not play. The investor shows a held pose (`REDUCED_MOTION_POSE`):

| Clip | Held at |
| --- | --- |
| Listening | 0 s (rest) |
| Explaining | 0.8 s (hand raised) |
| Offering | 0.8 s (reaching) |
| Withdrawal | 0.8 s (leaning back, frowning) |

A test checks that every held time falls inside its clip.

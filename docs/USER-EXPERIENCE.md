# User experience

Goal: immersive, entertaining, beginner-friendly entrepreneurship episode with visible consequences and meaningful choices. Current full practice is functional scripted UI;3D is a smaller feasibility slice.
Original eleven-scene plan: design/experience-definition.md and design/storyboard.html. Its2D art is composition/fallback reference, superseded as final direction by premium stylized 3D.

1. Cinematic landing: explain challenge and immediate entry.
2. Investor introductions: recognizable, selectable and skippable identities.
3. Guided pitch: plain-language business/financial help without losing drafts.
4. Review: cash/ownership/saving choices and explicit start.
5. Conversation: named speaker, readable text/composer and optional history.
6. Offers: committed reveal with all actual terms immediately accessible.
7. Negotiation: selection/comparison, nonbinding previews, remaining counters.
8. Confirmation: explicit current terms/session/revision before acceptance.
9. Outcome: distinguish deal, no offers, all withdrawn and walk-away respectfully.
10. Scorecard: factual consequences/decision review; future tested coaching.
11. Resume: validated save and truthful conflict/error state with settled scene.

No finance expertise or movement controls required. Keep readable HTML, keyboard equivalents, contrast/focus, reduced motion and portrait usability. Decoration cannot gate transactions or skip confirmation. Fallback preserves full approved functionality.
Do not invent startup viability, success/confidence grades or verification of founder data. Distinguish scripted/live operation honestly. Two fictional LunchLoop scenarios are documented in design/README.md; do not mix them.

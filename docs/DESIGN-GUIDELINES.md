# Design guidelines

Retain palette: background #101512, text #F2F1E9, muted #B2BAAA, lines #303A30, action lime #D5F27D. Local Instrument Serif headlines, Inter UI and IBM Plex Mono labels have OFL notices in src/app/fonts.
Final standard: modern premium narrative game, stylized adult investors, expressive faces, believable clothing/materials, premium architecture and cinematic comfortable lighting/cameras. Current graphics are temporary. Avoid copied television likenesses, excessive gradients/glass and generic dashboard grids.

Maya: precise finance, angular dark jacket, bob/brass detail. Adrian: energetic growth, copper curls, glasses/cobalt. Sofia: grounded operations, bun/teal/cream identity. Roles/statuses remain text and not color-only.
Use founder/panel/investor presets; interrupted transitions continue from current pose. No forced orbit/WASD. Reduced motion offers cuts/fixed framing and simplified mode.
Listening/questioning/explaining/offering/agreement/withdrawal form a compact pose vocabulary. Cues derive from committed game state. Real processing, not fake timers, drives future thinking indicators.
Prefer restrained lighting, local licensed assets/decoders and measured budgets. Finance stays readable screen-space HTML independent of light/camera.
Review long names, narrow portrait widths, real phones, keyboard, contrast, loading, failure and spoken assistive technology. Approve one cast-quality sample before producing all characters. Preserve source/provenance/redistribution records.
